Frequently Asked Questions

What are Fortnite Tokens on Champion Tokens?

Champion Tokens are competitive credits used on https://championtokens.fun to participate in 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 Fortnite Creative matches. Players can use tokens to unlock rare player titles, avatar cosmetics, and compete on the global leaderboard.

Is Champion Tokens safe and legitimate?

Yes. We use Discord's official OAuth 2.0 system for secure authentication. We never ask for or store passwords. For Fortnite matchmaking, we only use your public Epic Games display name so players can add each other in-game. Read more on our Trust & Safety page.

How do score disputes work?

If players disagree on a match outcome, the match enters Dispute status. Our 24/7 moderation team reviews submitted Fortnite replay files or video clips to verify the winner and award the prize pool.
