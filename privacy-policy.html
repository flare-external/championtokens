Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026 · Champion Tokens (championtokens.fun)
Champion Tokens values your privacy. This policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information when utilizing our competitive gaming platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, play matches, or interact with Champion Tokens, we collect the following information:

  • Authentication Data: When logging in through Discord OAuth, we collect your public Discord username, avatar, and Discord User ID.
  • Gaming Accounts: When linking your Epic Games account, we collect and store your official Epic Games / Fortnite display name and Account ID to facilitate competitive match lobbies and party invitations.
  • Match & Gameplay History: Records of matches created, joined, scores reported, victory ratios, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Token & Transaction Logs: Internal ledger records of tokens earned, match entry wagers, prize payouts, and cosmetic equipments.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly used to deliver and maintain platform features, specifically:

  • Creating, coordinating, and managing competitive 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 Fortnite matches.
  • Verifying player identities and displaying accurate Fortnite Epic IGNs to match participants.
  • Calculating win rates, seasonal rankings, and displaying the global leaderboard.
  • Distributing prize pool tokens automatically upon dual-confirmed match results.
  • Detecting fraudulent activities, multi-accounting, and resolving disputed match tickets.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Services

Champion Tokens integrates with trusted third-party providers:

  • Epic Games: We connect with Epic Games Account Services to verify your Fortnite player identity. We do not access, view, or store your Epic Games passwords.
  • Discord: Used strictly for secure authentication and player identification via OAuth2.
  • Firebase / Google Cloud: Powers our secure database infrastructure with strict security rules and encryption at rest and in transit.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All sensitive communication is encrypted via HTTPS / SSL, and administrative permissions are strictly restricted to verified moderators.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may request to disconnect your Epic Games account, update your display preferences, or request full deletion of your user account and transaction history by contacting our support team or opening a ticket in our official community server.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as new game modes and features are added. Changes are effective immediately upon posting to this page.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us via:

  • Website: https://championtokens.fun
  • Discord Support: Join our official support channel via the website footer.
