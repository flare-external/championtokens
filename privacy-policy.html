Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Champion Tokens (championtokens.fun)
Data Minimization Principle: We only collect what is strictly necessary to run matchmaking: your public Discord ID/Username, your public Epic Games IGN, and your match match statistics. We never see, store, or ask for your passwords, banking information, or physical address.

1. Information We Collect

We collect limited categories of information when you interact with Champion Tokens:

  • Discord Profile Information: When you log in via Discord OAuth2, Discord securely passes your public Discord User ID, username, email address, and profile avatar.
  • Epic Games Display Name (IGN): Your public in-game Fortnite username, entered voluntarily so opponents can send you in-game party invites.
  • Match & Gameplay History: Win/loss records, match scores, disputed replay logs, and leaderboard placement.
  • Transaction Ledger: Records of token entries, match prize winnings, cosmetic unlocks, and gift code redemptions.

2. Information We NEVER Collect

To ensure maximum privacy protection:

  • We NEVER collect or store your Discord password or Epic Games password.
  • We NEVER request two-factor authentication (2FA) codes or account recovery codes.
  • We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Coordinate real-time 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 match lobbies and tournament brackets.
  • Maintain the integrity of the global leaderboard and player statistics.
  • Verify dispute proof (replays/screenshots) and enforce fair play rules.
  • Prevent multi-accounting, unauthorized bots, and ban evasion.

4. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Protections)

Depending on your location, you hold fundamental data protection rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a complete export of all data associated with your user ID.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your Epic Games IGN or sync your latest Discord profile at any time in your Profile settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request full permanent deletion of your account, history, and records by emailing privacy@championtokens.fun or opening a Discord ticket.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Champion Tokens is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that an account belongs to a user under 13, we immediately terminate the account and purge associated data.

6. Security & Encryption Standards

All network communications use SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption. Database access is governed by strict Cloud Firestore Security Rules, ensuring users can only modify their own profile data.

7. Contact the Data Privacy Officer

For any privacy inquiries, data deletion requests, or compliance questions, please contact:

  • Email: privacy@championtokens.fun
  • Support Discord: https://discord.gg/35xqcMTEbH
