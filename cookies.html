Cookies & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Champion Tokens (championtokens.fun)

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and browser local storage technologies are small text files or key-value data structures stored on your device when you visit our website. They allow the platform to remember your active session, authenticate your Discord identity, and preserve your customized interface preferences.

2. Why We Use Strictly Essential Storage

Champion Tokens exclusively utilizes Strictly Necessary cookies and local storage tokens required for the platform to operate. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking networks.

Storage Item Type Purpose Duration
Firebase Auth JWT Essential Cookie / IndexedDB Maintains secure user login and encrypted session state. Session / 30 Days
ct_cookie_consent Local Storage Remembers your cookie banner preferences. 1 Year
ct_auth_sync_v Local Storage Synchronizes your latest Discord avatar and username. Persistent
ct_epic_linking_uid Local Storage Temporarily facilitates secure Epic Games OAuth linking. Session (Deleted post-link)

3. Third-Party Embeds & Services

Our platform interacts with select essential infrastructure providers:

  • Discord Inc.: Facilitates OAuth 2.0 authentication when you click "Login with Discord".
  • Google Cloud Firebase: Provides encrypted database synchronization and user authentication.
  • Cloudflare: Provides DDoS protection, SSL encryption, and edge content delivery.

4. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You can choose to block or delete cookies through your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Please note that because our cookies are strictly essential for authentication and balance tracking, disabling cookies may prevent you from logging into your Champion Tokens account.

5. Questions & Data Privacy Officer

For any questions regarding our use of cookies and local storage, please contact our Data Privacy Officer at privacy@championtokens.fun or visit our Privacy Policy.
