Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Champion Tokens Platform (championtokens.fun)
Summary: Uncontested match cancellations and expired lobbies immediately refund 100% of tokens to your wallet. Digital avatar cosmetics and title unlocks are permanent upon redemption. Disputes are resolved fairly by human moderators with replay verification.

1. Match Escrow & Cancellation Refunds

When you create or join a competitive match on Champion Tokens, your entry tokens are held in real-time atomic escrow:

  • Host Cancellation: If a match creator cancels an open lobby before an opponent joins, 100% of the token escrow is returned to the creator's balance immediately.
  • Expired Lobbies: Open match lobbies that remain unjoined for more than 30 minutes are automatically canceled, and all tokens are refunded to the host.
  • Mutual Cancellation: If both players agree to cancel the match before gameplay begins (due to incorrect Fortnite island codes, ping mismatch, or server issues), both players receive a full 100% refund.

2. Match Dispute Settlements

If a match result is disputed by either participant, the token prize pool remains locked in secure escrow until a human staff moderator reviews the game:

  • Valid Dispute / Proof Provided: If video recording or Fortnite replay files demonstrate a legitimate disconnect, server crash, or game-breaking bug before completion, the match is voided and all entry tokens are returned to both participants.
  • Rule Violations / Cheating: If a player is found guilty of cheating, exploiting, using third-party macros, or submitting fraudulent match scores, the violating player forfeits their entry fee, and the innocent player is awarded the match prize pool.

3. Digital Cosmetics & In-Game Titles

Purchases of digital cosmetics—including custom player avatars, player titles, and Champion Premium subscriptions—are digital goods delivered immediately to your user profile upon transaction. Because digital unlocks are consumed immediately:

  • Digital cosmetic purchases and title unlocks are non-refundable once applied to your profile.
  • Accidental duplicate purchases of active subscriptions are automatically credited or refunded upon detection.

4. Platform Tokens & Withdrawal Rules

Champion Tokens are digital platform units used exclusively for matchmaking participation and profile customization. If you experience an unexpected billing discrepancy or unauthorized account access, please open a support ticket immediately.

5. Contacting Support for Refund Inquiries

If you have any questions or require assistance regarding a match escrow refund or balance adjustment, contact our 24/7 support team:
