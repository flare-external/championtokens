Platform Safety, Privacy & Legitimacy

Champion Tokens is built from the ground up with industry-standard security protocols to protect your accounts, guarantee fair play, and ensure complete transparency.

Official Discord OAuth 2.0

Logins are processed securely on Discord's official servers. We never see, store, or ask for your password. Your credentials remain 100% private with Discord.

Public Epic IGNs Only

We only ask for your public Fortnite in-game display name so opponents can invite you to lobbies. We never request Epic Games passwords or 2FA codes.

100% Skill-Based Competition

All matches are pure esports competitions in Fortnite Creative (Realistic, Zone Wars, Box Fights). Results depend entirely on player mechanical skill, with zero chance elements.

Replay Auditing & Fair Play

Dedicated 24/7 staff moderators review in-game Fortnite replay files and video recordings for any dispute, ensuring zero tolerance for cheats or false reporting.

Safety & Security FAQ

Can Champion Tokens access or steal my Discord or Epic Games account?

No. Account theft is technically impossible. Logins are handled exclusively through Discord's official OAuth2 endpoint, where Discord grants an encrypted identity token without ever sharing your password. Furthermore, linking your Epic Games IGN only shares your public username so players can add each other in Fortnite.

Why does Champion Tokens provide a 1.00 free starter token?

We give every new player 1.00 free Champion Token so you can immediately test the matchmaking lobby, experience the competitive gameplay, and participate in matches without depositing any funds.

Is this gambling or a casino site?

No. Champion Tokens is strictly an electronic sports (esports) skill matchmaking platform. Players compete in Fortnite Creative matches where the outcome is determined entirely by building, editing, and shooting skill.
