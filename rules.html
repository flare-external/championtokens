Champion Tokens Competitive Rules

Please review and follow all tournament rules below. Failure to comply with platform guidelines will result in round forfeitures, match loss, or account penalties.

Availability & AFK Disconnections Connected Account Bugs & Glitches Lobby & Map Mode Specific Conduct & Staff Reporting

Availability & AFK

  • Players have a maximum of 10 minutes from the match starting to get into the lobby. If you aren't ready within this time, you will be forfeited.
  • If your opponent did not join within the given 10 minutes, you'll need to verify this by providing photo evidence to moderators.
  • In leaderboard tournaments, this maximum time for players to join the lobby is reduced to 5 minutes.
  • If teammates do not arrive in time, the match can proceed without them.
  • Each time an opponent goes AFK during the game, you can kill them a maximum of once.
  • Cancellation of the match can only occur if both parties agree.

Disconnections & Pauses

  • In all cases where a player goes AFK or disconnects midgame, the game can be paused for up to 6 minutes. After this period of time, the game must either continue or the absent player will be forfeited.
  • This 6 minute time period is cumulative per team. A player or teammate may leave more than once, as long as it does not exceed the 6 minute limit. Once the 6 minutes are up, the match must continue or the absent player will be forfeited.
  • To prevent siphon being awarded to opponents, you cannot respawn during a round.
  • If a team member leaves during a round, the round must continue without them and will be counted.
  • If a team member leaves before a round starts, the game is paused.

Connected Account

  • If a player has linked an Epic account with the incorrect platform or name, they have 5 minutes to rectify this from the time a Staff member arrives.
  • Upon fixing this issue, the game must be restarted using the correct account, but the current score line will remain the same.

Exploiting Bugs & Glitches

  • If a bug occurs during a round negatively affecting either player, the round will not count. If required, you may reset the game to fix the bug but resume from the current score line.
  • Anyone exploiting a bug for their own benefit will be punished.
  • Game crashes and other system errors are not treated as bugs, but as going AFK/disconnecting.

Lobby & Map Rules

  • The match must be played on the listed region and official island map code.
  • The host should play as Team 1 in game, whilst their opponents should play as Team 2. Rounds awarded to the incorrect team will count.
  • If the map is incorrect, you must resume on the correct map from the current score line.
  • Removing your opponent from the party during the game will result in a round loss.
  • Any rounds or matches played with incorrect game or host settings will still be considered valid and will count.
  • When the "No Simple Edit" option is selected (Simple Edit: Disabled), if any team member uses Simple Edit, their team will forfeit the entire match.

Gamemode Rules

Realistic Rules

1
Opening chests, ammo crates, collecting floor loot, or using slurp barrels during a round will lead to forfeiture of that round.
2
If a player leaves once loot has been distributed, the round must continue. Do not exit the match.
3
Respawning during an active round will result in forfeiting the round.
4
'Zone Wars' and 'Comp Mode' options must be enabled.

Boxfight Rules

1
When the "No Simple Edit" option is selected, if any team member uses Simple Edit, their team will forfeit the entire match.
2
You must not exploit bugs to keep extra loot from round to round.
3
If a player leaves once the barrier falls, the round must continue. Do not exit the match.
4
Respawning during an active round will result in forfeiting the round.

Zone Wars Rules

1
Standard competitive Zone Wars settings and siphon mechanics must be active.
2
If a player leaves once the round begins, the round must continue. Do not exit the match.
3
Respawning during an active round will result in forfeiting the round.
4
Teaming or griefing outside of designated squad allocation is strictly prohibited.

Miscellaneous & Sportsmanship

  • Spectators are not allowed. If a round is played with a spectator, they must be removed and the team that didn't invite the spectator will be granted a round win.
  • Toxicity is allowed. However, any forms of racism, homophobia, or hate speech will result in a platform mute. The win will be awarded to the user who won the match.
  • You must not attempt to deceive your opponent by lying about the match rules for your own advantage.
  • Staff will take into account the context and make appropriate decisions such as giving round wins or deciding an overall winner. This includes reasons such as time wasting, repeated disconnections without reason, unsportsmanlike conduct, etc.
  • All staff decisions are final and must be respected.

Reporting Opponents

  • If you're certain that your opponent has broken the rules, you should immediately stop playing and report your opponent.
  • If you're unsure, please play the match out and report them once you've finished the game.
Need moderator assistance during an active match? Click the "Call Staff" button inside your match lobby chat panel.
