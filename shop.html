Frequently Asked Questions
How much is 1 Champion Token worth?
1 Champion Token equals exactly $1.00 USD. Matches can be created for fractional token amounts (such as 0.50 tokens).
How do match payouts work?
When a match is created and joined, all player wagers form the total prize pool. The winning player or team receives 90% of the entire prize pool immediately.
What game modes can I play?
You can create or join 1v1, 2v2, or 3v3 matches in Realistic, Zone Wars, and Box Fights maps.
